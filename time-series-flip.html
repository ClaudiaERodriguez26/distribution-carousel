Time Series Analysis

Time series analysis is used to analyze and predict patterns in sequential data points over time. In marketing, this technique is commonly applied to understand historical trends and make predictions based on past performance.

Key Components of Time Series Analysis:

  • Trend: The long-term movement or direction of the data over time.
  • Seasonality: Regular patterns or fluctuations that occur at fixed intervals.
  • Noise: Random variations or irregularities in the data that cannot be attributed to specific factors.

Methods Used in Time Series Analysis:

  • Moving averages: Used to smooth out short-term fluctuations and highlight underlying trends.
  • Exponential smoothing: Assigning higher weighting to more recent data.
  • Autoregressive integrated moving average (ARIMA): A combination of autoregression, differencing, and moving averages to make predictions.
